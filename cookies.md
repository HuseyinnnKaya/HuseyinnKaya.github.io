## Understanding Cookies and HTTP Protocols

In modern web development, HTTP (HyperText Transfer Protocol) is stateless. This means a server does not automatically remember users between different requests.

### What is a Cookie?
A **cookie** is a small piece of data stored in your browser by a website. It allows the server to remember your session, preferences, or login status.

### Key Concepts
1. **Tracking Cookies:** These cookies follow users across multiple websites to deliver targeted information. Modern browsers (like Safari and Firefox) often block these by default.
2. **Data Privacy (GDPR & KVKK):** Modern web applications are legally required to ask users for permission before storing tracking cookies.
3. **Encrypted Connections (HTTPS):** HTTPS uses digital certificates to encrypt data sent between the browser and the server, ensuring privacy and data integrity.
